Tomato Early Blight

Alternaria solani

Fungal Moderate risk

What is it?

info

Early blight is a common fungal disease that affects tomatoes, potatoes, and peppers. It primarily targets older leaves first, creating concentric "target" spots that eventually lead to leaf drop and reduced fruit yield.

Affected Crops

nutrition
Tomato

Symptoms

  • check_circle Dark brown spots with concentric rings.
  • check_circle Yellowing of leaves around the dark spots.
  • check_circle Premature defoliation of lower canopy.
